Frequently asked questions

Everything about orders, delivery, bulk pricing for schools, returns and accounts.

Orders & delivery

Track, change and receive your orders.

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days. School bulk orders within the metro area are delivered in 48 hours. Orders over $250 ship free.
Can I track my order?
Yes — once your order ships you'll get an email with a tracking link. You can also view live status in your account under "My Orders".
Can I change or cancel an order?
You can change or cancel within 2 hours of placing it. After that it enters picking and packing. Contact us ASAP and we'll do our best.

Bulk & schools

Pricing, invoicing and recurring orders.

When does bulk pricing apply?
Bulk tiers start at 50 units of a single item and deepen at 100 and 250 units. The best price applies automatically in your cart.
Can schools be invoiced?
Yes — registered school accounts can choose invoice billing at checkout with net-30 terms after a quick verification.
Can I set up a recurring school order?
Absolutely. Our school orders desk can schedule termly recurring deliveries — just contact us with your list.

Returns & refunds

Our 30-day return promise.

What's your return policy?
Unopened items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened packs may be returned for store credit if defective.
How do I start a return?
Go to "My Orders", pick the order and click "Return". We'll email a prepaid label for orders over $50.

Accounts

Signing in, security and rewards.

I forgot my password — what now?
Use the forgot password page and we'll email a secure reset link valid for 30 minutes.
How do rewards points work?
You earn 1 point per $1 spent on Starter accounts. 100 points = $1 off a future order. Points never expire while your account is active.

Couldn't find your answer?

Our team is happy to help with any question, big or small.
