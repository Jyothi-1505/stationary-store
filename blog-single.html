The Ultimate Back-to-School Supply Checklist (2025)

Elena RiveraAug 12, 2025 · 8 min read

After 15 years of packing school orders, we've seen which supplies actually get used — and which gather dust. Here's the no-fluff checklist we hand every parent and teacher who walks into our shop.

Kindergarten & Pre-K

Little hands need big, safe tools. A jumbo crayon set, safety scissors, two glue sticks and a drawing pad cover 90% of what early-years teachers ask for. Skip the expensive branded folders — plain ones work just as well.

Elementary (Grades 1–5)

The workhorse years. You'll want a 24-pack of pre-sharpened pencils, three ruled notebooks, five pocket folders and a sturdy pencil case. Add a highlighter pack once your child hits grade 3 — they start using them for reading groups.

"Buy two extra pencil cases. One will disappear by October — they always do." — Aisha, School Accounts

Middle School (Grades 6–8)

Math gets serious. A geometry set and a scientific calculator are non-negotiable. Graph paper by the ream, plus a dedicated math notebook, keep equations tidy. Lockers mean a combination lock is worth adding.

High School (Grades 9–12)

Subject-specific supplies rule here. Index cards for vocab, a lab notebook for science, and premium pens for the essays that count. A planner — paper or digital — is the single biggest predictor of staying organized.
