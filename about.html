Quick FAQ

Do you offer school bulk discounts?
Yes — order 50+ units of any item to unlock tiered pricing. See our bulk pricing page for full tiers.
How fast is bulk delivery?
School bulk orders are delivered within 48 hours within the metro area and 3–5 days nationwide.
Can I set up a recurring school order?
Absolutely. Our school orders desk can schedule termly recurring deliveries — just contact us.
