Our Journey

Growing With Every Learning Journey

From a small neighborhood bookstore to a trusted educational partner, every milestone reflects our commitment to making quality learning resources accessible to everyone.

2014

First School Partnership

We partnered with our first educational institution and began supplying complete classroom essentials in bulk.

Absolutely. Our school orders desk can schedule termly recurring deliveries — just contact us.
