Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2025. This is demo content for template preview purposes.

1. Introduction

Scholar Stationery ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. This document is template placeholder content and should be reviewed by a legal professional before use.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as name, email, shipping address and order details — when you create an account or place an order. We also collect usage data like pages visited and products viewed to improve your shopping experience.

3. How we use information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To provide bulk pricing and invoice billing for schools.
  • To send order updates and, with your consent, marketing offers.
  • To improve our catalog, website and customer service.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to opt out of marketing at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at our contact page.

5. Data security

We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption for account data and never store full payment card numbers. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart, and optional analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

7. Third parties

We share data only with trusted partners required to fulfil orders — payment processors and delivery carriers — under strict data agreements. We never sell your personal information.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@scholarstationery.com or write to 42 Maple Avenue, Riverton. We'll respond within five business days.
