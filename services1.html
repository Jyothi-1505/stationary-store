Shopping FAQ

When does bulk pricing apply?
Bulk tiers start at 50 units of a single item and deepen at 100 and 250 units. The best price applies automatically in your cart.
Can schools be invoiced?
Yes — registered school accounts can choose invoice billing at checkout with net-30 terms.
Do you restock sold-out items?
We restock weekly. Use the "notify me" option on any product page and we'll email you when it's back.

Need a school bulk quote?

Send us your list and we'll reply with a tailored quote within one business day.
